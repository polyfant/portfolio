02 — Track your Foal · gestation tracker

Three hundred
forty days.

A mare carries her foal for roughly 340 days. The app walks breeders through every milestone — the early scan, the heartbeat, the final watch — with reminders and a backup account, so nothing is lost when the phone isn't. Built with Expo and React Native; signed release pipeline straight to Android.

Version one shipped as a Go + PostgreSQL backend. Version two is a native-first rewrite — the lesson being: meet mare owners on their phones, where the stable actually is.
